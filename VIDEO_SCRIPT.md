## Demo Tips

### Preparation
1. Have MetaMask connected to Polygon
2. Clear browser cache for fresh demo
3. Test all ENS names beforehand
4. Prepare backup ENS names in case of issues
5. Have network RPC URLs configured

### During Demo
- Speak clearly and maintain energy
- Highlight sponsor integrations naturally
- Show mobile responsiveness if time permits
- Have error scenarios ready as backup
- Keep slides/talking points minimal - let the app shine
